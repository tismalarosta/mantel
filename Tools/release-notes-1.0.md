First release of Mantel: one menu bar app that combines Vorssaint, Boring Notch, DockDoor, Ice and Mac Duo, plus Type Clipboard. Every feature is a switch, so you turn on only what you want.

**Install:** download `Mantel.zip`, unzip it and drag Mantel into Applications. macOS says it can't check the app, because Mantel is not signed by a registered Apple developer: click Done, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. The zip has a short note in English and French with the same steps.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or newer. Mantel doesn't update itself, new versions land here.

Mantel is a fork of [Vorssaint](https://github.com/vorssaint/vorssaint-utils) and is not affiliated with it or with the other projects it builds on. Credits and licences are in the [README](https://github.com/tismalarosta/mantel#readme).
