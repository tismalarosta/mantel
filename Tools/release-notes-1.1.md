Mantel 1.1 adds two switches for using a Mac with the lid shut, and Mantel now checks for its own updates.

**New**
- Stay online: with the lid shut and Keep Awake on, Mantel keeps the Mac on the best Wi-Fi from your list, falls back to your iPhone's hotspot when none is in range, and moves back as soon as one returns.
- Pause apps with the lid closed: while the lid is shut, every Dock app you didn't keep is frozen and picks up where it was when you open the lid.
- Updates: Mantel checks this page and tells you when a new version is out. Turn on "Install updates for me" to let it download and replace the app itself.

Both lid switches are off until you turn them on in Features.

**Fixed**
- The Dock and Finder show Mantel's icon instead of Vorssaint's.
- On macOS 27, a click on the menu bar panel's first tab no longer closes the panel.
- On macOS 27, the menu bar hider stays off, because macOS 27 rebuilt the menu bar and the hider can no longer hide items there. Use System Settings, Menu Bar, Allow in the Menu Bar instead.

**Install or update:** 1.0 can't update itself, so this time download `Mantel.zip`, unzip it and drag Mantel into Applications, replacing the old copy. macOS says it can't check the app, because Mantel is not signed by a registered Apple developer: click Done, then go to System Settings, Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. The zip has a short note in English and French with the same steps.

Needs a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or newer.
